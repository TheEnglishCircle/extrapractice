Roll → move → solve → return to the board. First to FINISH wins!
🎲
How to play

Take turns rolling the dice. Read your challenge aloud, discuss your answer and select it. Correct answers earn 1 point; incorrect answers earn 0 points. Your token stays on its new space either way. Each challenge has a short explanation. You must finish the challenge before the next player rolls.

Teacher tip: Before choosing, ask the player to explain their choice. Other players may challenge their reasoning. The board includes original questions modelled on the grammar and exercise formats in Unit 9.1.
